◆ Hospital Playlist Season 1 & 2 (슬기로운 의사생활 시즌 1 & 2)

Chae Songhwa's Office Rendered
Day Version

Dedicated to one of my favorite K-dramas, Hospital Playlist, this Blender diorama recreates Chae Songhwa's office at Yulje Medical Center, the heart of the show where the five best friends always gather.
Note: Scenes 3 and 4 are original concepts I designed myself.


◆ Thirty, Nine (서른, 아홉)

Jung Chanyoung's Living Room
Angled View

Recreating the cozy ambience of Jung Chanyoung's living room from the Korean TV drama series Thirty, Nine. In this diorama, I focused on the light setup and composition in Blender, with special attention given to the curtains and overall environment.


◆ Hospital Playlist Season 2 (슬기로운 의사생활 시즌 2)

Chae Songhwa's Bedroom
Day Version

A full view of Chae Songhwa's bedroom in 1999. Working on this diorama taught me how to efficiently scatter and manage repetitive objects, saving time while allowing me to detail individual, unique books.
