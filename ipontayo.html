IponTayo

An AI-integrated personal finance app addressing low financial literacy in the Philippines — designed to make net-pay calculation, budgeting, and localized saving habits like Paluwagan and Padala easy to track in one place.

01

The Problem

Only a quarter of Filipinos are confident with basic budgeting, while most financial apps on the market are foreign-made and don't reflect local money habits — things like Paluwagan (community savings), Padala (family remittances), or the specific structure of PH payroll deductions (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, BIR TRAIN tax).

IponTayo set out to close that gap: an app that speaks the language of Filipino personal finance instead of asking users to translate their habits into a generic budgeting template.

04

Process

  • 01

    Systems Analysis

    Worked from the team's SWOT, use case diagram, DFDs, and ERD to understand the actual data and process requirements before opening Figma.

  • 02

    Hi-Fi Design

    Designed 5 high-fidelity mobile screens covering net pay calculation, budgeting, and goal-tracking interfaces.

  • 03

    Usability Walkthroughs

    Had 5+ classmates and a professor navigate the prototype live, gathering feedback that shaped the final iteration (e.g., decluttering the dashboard by removing the currency exchange feature).

05

Outcome

The final prototype was approved with minor formatting adjustments only — icon, spacing, and color refinements — with the core flows and feature set validated as-is after the usability walkthroughs.
